The story

Every gathering has a ritual.

Long before nightlife, people gathered around a shared cup: cacao in the Americas, tea across Asia, resin and rose in the ancient markets. The drink and the scent were part of the ceremony.

COSMOTION Potions brings that back to the gatherings of now. Something beautiful to hold that keeps you present, a cup to open the circle, a scent the room remembers. Made with real botanicals and honest words, without hype or miracle promises.

We are all made of cosmos.

Our standards

Honest in every bottle.

Proposed commitments, to be confirmed with formulators and manufacturing partners.

Proposed
IngredientsReal botanicals, every one listed on the label
FormulationDeveloped with qualified food scientists, herbalists and perfumers
ProductionMade by licensed manufacturers and co-packers
Alcohol-freeThe elixir line launches alcohol-free
SourcingNo endangered plants such as wild white sage or palo santo
PackagingGlass and refillable formats where possible

We'll describe these products by taste, scent and ritual, not by health benefits. All wording will be reviewed against food and cosmetic regulations before launch.
