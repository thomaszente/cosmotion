The story

A garment for the moments that matter.

For centuries the kimono has been worn for ceremony, celebration and passage. COSMONOS brings that idea into the gatherings of now: desert nights, island sunsets, after-hours rooms and the long morning after the music.

It's the energy of Burning Man, Tulum and Mykonos, of underground electronic music and of ancient civilizations, distilled into something quiet and enduring. Not festival merch. Not a costume. A piece you keep, pass on and return to.

We are all made of cosmos.

Artist editions

Every print, a collaboration.

Alongside the founding collections, each season would invite one visual artist to create a print for a strictly limited, numbered edition, credited by name and shared with the artist.

COSMOTION already works with visual artists, performers and producers across its events. Artist editions extend that circle into something wearable and collectible.
