What COSMOTION is

Not an event company. Not only a label. A creative ecosystem.

What began in 2015 as a passion for bringing people together through music has grown into a platform for immersive events, electronic music, artist development, creative direction, venue development and community building.

Cosmos+Motion=Cosmotion

Kosmos is Greek for order and harmony. Motio is Latin for movement. Order alone is a frozen star chart; motion alone scatters. Together they make an orbit: a center that holds, and everything around it free to move.

What we believe

Music is more than entertainment

It's a universal language that connects people, sparks creativity and makes room for real change. Some of life's most meaningful moments happen when people are fully present, lose themselves in music and feel part of something larger. Nine principles guide everything we make.

Many of our dance floors have been no-photo spaces. Phones away, so the night is lived rather than filmed.

What we create

Five pillars, one orbit

Each part feeds the others. The events find the artists, the artists shape the label, the label fills the rooms, and the community holds it all together. Tap a pillar.

Our story

From underground gatherings to an ecosystem

2015The first gatherings

A room, a sound system, the right people

Thomas Zente, who performs as ZENTI, founds COSMOTION in Austin out of a simple love of bringing people together through music.

UndergroundThe formative years

Learning what a night can be

Underground events, after-hours, festival experiences and unconventional venues. More than 100 events taught us how environment, music and intention work together.

Wider orbitsBeyond Austin

Sharing the floor with the world

Collaborations with international artists, and ZENTI's sets carrying the sound to festivals from Burning Man to Envision.

SpacesBuilding rooms

Two venue launches

Taking everything learned on dance floors into building places of our own, most recently NOWHERE in the St. Elmo Arts District in 2026.

2026January

The label's first official release, CM001 "Boomin' Now", marks the start of a catalog for music that moves body and spirit.
