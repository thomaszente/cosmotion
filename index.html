Cosmos+Motion=Cosmotion

Kosmos is Greek for order, harmony and beauty. Motio is Latin for movement. Order alone is a frozen star chart; motion alone scatters. Together they make an orbit: a center that holds, and everything around it free to flow.

That is how we guide an artist. Your vision is the center. Strategy, sound and story move around it.
